# Privacy Policy for Top 10 Bhajans

**Effective Date:** November 16, 2026

Top 10 Bhajans ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Android application handles user information.

## 1. Information We Collect
We do **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII), analytics, or usage tracking data on external servers. 

## 2. Local Device Storage
Any bhajans you mark as "Favorites" are saved locally on your own device using Android's built-in storage. This data never leaves your phone and is deleted automatically if you clear app data or uninstall the app.

## 3. Third-Party Content (YouTube)
Our app embeds official devotional videos from YouTube using the official YouTube IFrame Player. When you play a video, YouTube may collect technical data (such as your IP address or device information) in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://google.com) and [YouTube's Terms of Service](https://youtube.com).

## 4. Children's Privacy
Our app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.
